01 / Featured Project MULTIMODAL AI

Multimodal Toxic Speech Detection

Developed a multimodal deep learning system to automatically detect toxic, abusive and offensive speech in Hindi-English code-mixed video content by jointly analyzing textual, speech and visual modalities.

TextIndicBERT for multilingual text understanding and abusive-language classification.
SpeechWhisper and Wav2Vec 2.0 for transcription and speech feature extraction.
VisualVGG16 and OpenCV for visual/video feature analysis.
FusionLate-fusion of multimodal features for final classification.
  • Built an end-to-end pipeline using Python, PyTorch, Hugging Face Transformers, OpenCV, Whisper, FFmpeg and Librosa.
  • Performed video preprocessing, speech transcription, audio processing, frame extraction and multimodal feature extraction.
  • Integrated IndicBERT, Wav2Vec 2.0 and VGG16 to analyze text, speech and visual information.
  • Implemented late-fusion techniques to combine multimodal representations for toxicity and abusive content classification.
  • Evaluated model performance using Accuracy, Precision, Recall and F1-Score.
  • Designed the system with potential applications in real-world content moderation and harmful-content detection.
  • Featured in Dainik Bhaskar's DB Star — see the news coverage ↓
