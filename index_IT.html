Employee Attendance System

An end-to-end attendance system for office entry points. A camera recognises an employee's face, the server records check-in or check-out against their shift, and admins are alerted when an unknown face appears. Built during my internship at Umbrella Protection Systems.

Edge reader

  • Reads from a webcam or RTSP camera, with reconnect and exponential backoff on failure.
  • Detects faces with HOG and a Haar cascade fallback, encodes them, and suppresses repeats within a 30-second window.
  • Saves annotated snapshots and retries uploads to the server.

Server and attendance

  • Registration validates name, email and mobile, rejects duplicates, and stores a face encoding per image.
  • Recognition flips each image for a second encoding and needs two stored images under the 0.54 distance threshold before it confirms a match.
  • Check-in and check-out rules: check-in comes first, repeated actions are blocked, and each shift is marked early, late, overtime or early leave.
  • Unknown faces trigger Firebase push notifications to admins.
